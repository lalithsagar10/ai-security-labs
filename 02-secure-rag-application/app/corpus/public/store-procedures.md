# Store Opening and Closing Procedures

Owner: Store Operations · Last reviewed: 2026-07-20

## Opening
Arrive 30 minutes before opening. Disarm the alarm with your personal code, count the cash drawer
float ($200), and check the overnight Order Desk queue for in-store pickups.

## Closing
Count drawers with a second staff member present. Drop cash over $200 in the safe. Lock the stock
room and set the alarm. The closing manager signs the daily close log.

## Incidents
Report any safety incident, theft or customer injury to the store manager immediately and log it
in the Incident Portal within 24 hours.
