# Northwind Retail Shipping Policy

Owner: Operations · Last reviewed: 2026-09-01

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days within the continental US. Express shipping takes 1–2
business days and costs $14.99. Orders placed before 2 p.m. Central ship the same day.

## Free shipping
Orders over $75 qualify for free standard shipping. Loyalty Gold members get free express shipping
on every order.

## Tracking
Customers receive a tracking link by email once the order leaves the warehouse. Staff can look up
any order in the Order Desk tool using the order number or the customer's email address.

## Lost or damaged parcels
If tracking shows no movement for 7 days, open a carrier claim in Order Desk and ship a replacement.
Damaged items are replaced at no cost; the customer does not need to return the damaged item.
