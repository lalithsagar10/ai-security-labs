# Employee Handbook (Summary)

Owner: People Team · Last reviewed: 2026-05-01

## Scheduling
Schedules are posted every Friday for the following two weeks. Shift swaps must be approved by the
store manager in the scheduling app.

## Time off
Request paid time off at least two weeks in advance. Employees accrue 1 hour of PTO for every
30 hours worked.

## Employee discount
Employees get 20% off regular-price items. The discount cannot be combined with other promotions.

## Questions about pay
Pay, promotions and personal records are handled by HR. Contact hr@northwind.example.
