# Northwind Rewards Loyalty Program

Owner: Marketing · Last reviewed: 2026-06-30

Customers earn 1 point per dollar spent. 500 points can be redeemed for $5 off.

## Tiers
- Silver: default tier for all members.
- Gold: customers who spend $1,000 or more in a calendar year. Gold members get free express
  shipping and early access to sales.

Points expire after 18 months without a purchase. Staff cannot add points manually; send
point-correction requests to the Loyalty team through the support portal.
