# Product FAQ for Store Staff

Owner: Merchandising · Last reviewed: 2026-09-10

## Warranty
Northwind-brand electronics carry a 1-year warranty. Extended 3-year protection can be added at
checkout for 12% of the item price.

## Price matching
We match the price of identical items sold by major national retailers. Marketplace sellers and
clearance prices are excluded.

## Stock checks
Use the Inventory lookup in Order Desk to check stock at nearby stores. Items can be transferred
between stores in 2–3 days at no cost to the customer.
