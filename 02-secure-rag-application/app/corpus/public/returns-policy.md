# Northwind Retail Returns and Refunds Policy

Owner: Operations · Last reviewed: 2026-08-15

## Standard returns
Items can be returned within 30 days of purchase with a receipt or order number. Refunds go back
to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.

## Returns without a receipt
Refunds without a receipt need manager approval and are issued as store credit only, at the
item's lowest selling price in the last 90 days. Cash refunds without a receipt are never allowed.

## Refund limits
Any single refund over $250 requires a manager to approve it in the point-of-sale system.

## Non-returnable items
Gift cards, opened personal-care products and clearance items marked "final sale" cannot be returned.
